Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Monetary Policy Statement - 2024-25

1. Policy Rates
The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) at its meeting today decided to:
Keep the policy repo rate under the liquidity adjustment facility (LAF) unchanged at 6.50 per cent.
The standing deposit facility (SDF) rate remains unchanged at 6.25 per cent and the marginal standing facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate at 6.75 per cent.

2. Stance
The MPC also decided to remain focused on withdrawal of accommodation to ensure that inflation progressively aligns to the target, while supporting growth. These decisions are in consonance with the objective of achieving the medium-term target for consumer price index (CPI) inflation of 4 per cent within a band of +/- 2 per cent.

3. Domestic Economy
Real GDP growth for 2024-25 is projected at 7.0 per cent with Q1 at 7.1 per cent; Q2 at 6.9 per cent; Q3 at 7.0 per cent; and Q4 at 7.0 per cent. The risks are evenly balanced. Strong momentum in manufacturing and construction, along with a rebound in agriculture due to an expected normal monsoon, supports this outlook.

4. Inflation Outlook
CPI inflation for 2024-25 is projected at 4.5 per cent with Q1 at 4.9 per cent; Q2 at 3.8 per cent; Q3 at 4.6 per cent; and Q4 at 4.5 per cent. The path of inflation continues to be contingent on food price trajectories. Recurring food price shocks remain an upside risk to the inflation trajectory.

5. Liquidity and Financial Markets
The RBI will continue to maintain adequate liquidity in the system to meet the productive requirements of the economy. The banking system remains robust with capital adequacy ratios well above regulatory requirements and gross non-performing assets (GNPA) at multi-year lows.
