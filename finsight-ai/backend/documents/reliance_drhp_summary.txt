Reliance Retail Ventures Limited - Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) Summary

1. Business Overview
Reliance Retail Ventures Limited (RRVL) is the retail initiative of the Reliance group and is central to our consumer facing businesses. It has firmly established itself as the undisputed leader in the Indian retail market. Our business operates primarily across four key consumption baskets: Consumer Electronics, Fashion & Lifestyle, Grocery, and Pharma.

2. Financial Performance (FY23)
For the financial year ended March 31, 2023, RRVL reported a consolidated gross revenue of ₹2,60,364 crore, showcasing a robust year-on-year growth. The EBITDA for the period stood at ₹17,928 crore, reflecting improved operational efficiencies and scale benefits. The net profit attributable to the company was ₹9,181 crore.

3. Expansion Strategy
Our strategy focuses on aggressive omnichannel expansion. We aim to integrate our extensive network of physical stores with our digital commerce platforms (JioMart, Ajio, Reliance Digital). Over the next three years, we plan to increase our footprint in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities by opening 2,000 new stores and expanding our warehousing capacity by 30%.

4. Risk Factors
a) Intense competition from both domestic and international retail players (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, Tata Neu) could affect market share and margins.
b) Supply chain disruptions and inventory management risks are significant given our vast scale.
c) Changes in Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) policies in retail and e-commerce could impact operational structures.

5. Use of Proceeds
The net proceeds from the proposed IPO will be utilized for:
- Repayment or prepayment of certain borrowings (approx. ₹3,500 crore)
- Funding capital expenditure for new store openings and supply chain infrastructure (approx. ₹4,000 crore)
- General corporate purposes and strategic acquisitions.
